  the algae_boost feature: it never actually worked, for two
  independent reasons. First, mizer's project_simple() treats params

  threaded forward between timesteps) - reefDegrade()'s algae_boost
  branch tried to persist a boosted algae_growth/algae_capacity value
  by mutating params, which could never survive to the next timestep.
  Second, the bundled models stored their baseline algae growth rate
  under the stale field name algae_growth_initial (same old-porting-

  (data-only, same technique as above) and rebuilding the boost as a
